### Nyumba: a growing project

### Custom Trade Assistant: an honest test bench for trading ideas

Repository [thyybot-dev/custom-trade-assistant](https://github.com/thyybot-dev/custom-trade-assistant), reviewed 29 October 2026.

**What it is.** A research system that started as a Nasdaq momentum and order-block indicator ("Momentum OB") and is turning into a product: a local-first test bench where any trader brings their candles and their rule and gets an honest verdict in plain words. It reports trades after real costs, whether the result beats luck, whether it holds on data the rule never saw, and what a prop-firm challenge would likely do. The stated goal is to get people ready for a funded prop-firm account honestly, not to promise riches.

**What is built (in 12 days).**

- **Commits:** 410 since 17 October 2026
- **Code:** about 26,000 lines of Python and 2,400 of MQL5
- **Pieces:** MT5 indicator and monitor EAs; a Python reference detector with parity checks against MQL5; a strategy factory; exit, filter, news and time-of-day studies; Monte Carlo and prop-firm simulators (FTMO-style rules); a Replay Trainer page; a phone-first "Project Brain" dashboard
- **Evidence:** 227 tested results, 124 strategies in the library, 69 data sets
- **Discipline:** versioned specs, sealed "vault" data, random-entry baselines, reproducible run records, an AI-spend cap, and a rule that the core never depends on AI
- **Users:** none outside the founder yet

**Honest results so far.** The lead strategy on the Nasdaq (US100) shows 70 trades at about 0.2 R per trade, too few trades to prove an edge. The original order-block strategy loses money on EURNZD (219 trades, about −0.3 R per trade). The repository's own documents put prop-firm challenge pass rates at about 5–14%.

**What it shows about Emlyn.** This is his most disciplined software so far: specs before code, parity and correctness tests, sealed test data, scripted repeatable work, and a builder-reviewer loop between two AI agents. The same discipline would close the release gap in Formcraft's code.

**How it fits.**

- **Validate before building more:** the repository's own plan says to show the verdict card to five to ten serious traders and see whether they would pay. That is the next step, not more features.

**Chosen path.** The Trade Assistant stays a personal tool and Emlyn's route to a funded prop-firm account, not a product or a group company for now. In 2027 the order is Formcraft first, then Nyumba groundwork, then trading.
