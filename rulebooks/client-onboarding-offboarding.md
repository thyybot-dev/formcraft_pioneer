# Client onboarding and offboarding playbooks

The same steps run in the Command Centre (page: Client on and offboarding). Day = days after the journey starts.

## Architectural design (Studio)

### Onboarding

| Day | Step | Owner | Use | Automation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Log the enquiry, qualify it and send the auto-reply | Sales | Enquiries | Auto-reply and lead score |
| 1 | Book the discovery call or site visit from the free calendar slots | Sales | Calendar | Auto-booking |
| 1 | Send the design brief questionnaire | Studio lead | FMC-STU-BRF | Auto-send |
| 3 | Collect client details: ID or company papers, TIN, land title or lease copy, contacts | Admin | Clients register | Client form |
| 3 | Send the fee proposal and scope (RIBA stages, deliverables, revisions included) | Studio lead | FMC-SLS-PRP | AI drafts from the brief |
| 5 | Sign the design services agreement (fees, stages, IP and licence of drawings, data consent) | Director | Contracts register |  |
| 7 | Issue the deposit invoice and confirm payment and receipt | Finance | Invoice, receipt | Payment confirmation and auto-receipt |
| 7 | Create the project, its folder and the client portal access | Studio lead | Project hub | Auto-create on payment |
| 7 | Send the welcome pack: team, timeline, how we communicate, meeting rhythm, what we need from the client | Studio lead | Welcome pack | AI drafts |
| 10 | Hold the kick-off meeting and send the minutes | Studio lead | Meetings | AI minutes |
| 14 | Order the survey and soil test where needed | Studio lead | Purchase orders |  |

### Offboarding

| Day | Step | Owner | Use | Automation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Issue the final drawings with a transmittal | Studio lead | Drawings register | Auto-transmittal |
| 7 | Support the building permit submission and record the approval | Studio lead | Permits register |  |
| 7 | Send the final invoice and confirm the balance is paid | Finance | Invoice, receipt | Payment reminders |
| 10 | Hand over the agreed files (PDF, and native files if the contract says so) | Studio lead | Transmittal |  |
| 14 | Hold the close-out meeting | Studio lead | Meetings | AI minutes |
| 14 | Send the feedback survey (score and comments) | Sales | Feedback register | Auto-send |
| 21 | Ask for a testimonial, photos and permission to publish the case study | Sales | Media register | AI drafts |
| 30 | Ask for referrals and offer construction (design-and-build) where it fits | Sales | Deals | Reminder |
| 30 | Archive the project files and close the project record | Admin | Project hub |  |
| 60 | Delete personal data no longer needed (Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019) | Admin | Clients register | Reminder |

## Construction and design-and-build

### Onboarding

| Day | Step | Owner | Use | Automation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Qualify the enquiry and agree the scope, site and budget range | Sales | Enquiries | Auto-reply |
| 3 | Visit the site and record photos, access, services and risks | Site lead | Site diary |  |
| 10 | Prepare the BOQ, price and programme; send the quotation | QS | FMC-QSB BOQ, quotation | AI drafts from the model |
| 10 | Collect client details, TIN and land title; check who signs and who pays | Admin | Clients register | Client form |
| 14 | Sign the construction contract (payment schedule, retention, defects period, variations, insurance) | Director | Contracts register |  |
| 14 | Receive any guarantees and confirm Contractor's All Risks insurance | Finance | Insurance |  |
| 14 | Issue the mobilisation invoice and confirm the advance payment | Finance | Invoice, receipt | Payment confirmation |
| 18 | Confirm the building permit and display it on site | Site lead | Permits register |  |
| 21 | Take possession of the site; set up hoarding, signboard and site office | Site lead | Site diary |  |
| 21 | Run the safety induction for every worker | Site lead | FMC-ADM-IND | Induction checklist |
| 21 | Set up the client portal: programme, progress photos, payments | Project manager | Project hub | Auto weekly report |
| 21 | Hold the kick-off meeting and agree the site meeting rhythm | Project manager | Meetings | AI minutes |

### Offboarding

| Day | Step | Owner | Use | Automation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Inspect for practical completion with the client and record the snag list | Project manager | Defects register |  |
| 14 | Close every snag and get the client to sign off | Site lead | Defects register | Reminders |
| 14 | Hand over as-built drawings, O&M manuals, warranties and test certificates | Project manager | Drawings, files |  |
| 14 | Hand over keys with a signed handover certificate | Project manager | Handover certificate |  |
| 21 | Agree the final account (variations included) | QS | Variations register |  |
| 21 | Release half the retention at practical completion | Finance | Invoice |  |
| 21 | Demobilise: clear the site and return plant | Site lead | Plant register |  |
| 30 | Send the feedback survey; ask for a testimonial, photos and the case study | Sales | Feedback, media | Auto-send |
| 180 | Defects period check at 6 months | Project manager | Defects register | Calendar reminder |
| 365 | Final inspection; release the remaining retention | Finance | Invoice | Calendar reminder |
| 380 | Archive the project; delete personal data no longer needed | Admin | Project hub | Reminder |
